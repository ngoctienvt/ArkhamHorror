Master Shot
16:9 widescreen at 1536×864 or larger. Create a panoramic high-angle view of the Enchanted Woods of the Dreamlands at twilight, one continuous Lovecraftian oil-painted game map. The ancient woods are dense, with tangled roots and a winding amber-lit path running from the upper center down the middle. Use the canopy tunnel of roots from the Enchanted Path card at the top center.

Attachment 2 = Enchanted Woods: Stone Trapdoor (06053), woodland and undergrowth reference. The six woodland positions will be shuffled during play, so use only its woodland character and omit its unique trapdoor. Place exactly six generic distinct grassy clearings: upper, middle and lower left plus upper, middle and lower right. All six glades must be clearly visible from above, separated organically by dense trees, well within the frame and connected by narrow trails to the central path. Clear the six glade centers for overlays; no painted circles, doors, buildings, traps, paths in a grid, or props unique to any one card.
Lighting
