Master Shot
A 16:9, 1536×864 or larger Lovecraftian oil painting to serve as a high-angle game map of the summit of Kadath. Show one seamless snow-covered mountain ridge. The Onyx Castle is a central dark blue-black complex, with extremely tall slender faceted onyx spires as in attachment 1. Aerial perspective high enough to show the full footprint of the buildings and the four positions.
Spatial Landmarks
Attachment 1 = Onyx Castle at the exact CENTER. Attachment 2 = revealed Forsaken Tower, the actual tower stone shapes: almost black cylindrical stonework, narrow upright pillars and slitted windows. Build exactly four matching detached narrow round towers, each with the same thin slits and the same slightly broken flat crown. Their little rock outcrops are clearly separate from the central castle and are at x50% y18% NORTH, x50% y80% SOUTH, x18% y50% WEST, x82% y50% EAST. Keep the north tower visible in full behind the smaller central footprint, and the south tower in front. One thin bridge runs out to each tower, each outcrop with clear space for a map pin. Attachment 3 = unrevealed Forsaken Tower card; only use its dim purple tones, exclude the crystal ball, figures and every card graphic. Exactly four detached towers. No other detached towers or corner buildings.
Lighting
Cold blue and purple mountain night with restrained silver snow and faint olive highlights grazing the dark tower stones. Atmospheric, consistent perspective. Avoid comic panels, split screen, multi-panel, grid, borders, frames, text labels, watermark, cartoonish, 3D plastic, people.
