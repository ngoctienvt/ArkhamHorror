# Carcosa story illustrations

The illustrated campaign pages cover the prologue, opening and resolution branches of all eight scenarios, the two interludes, and the epilogue for both Carcosa campaigns. The [passage map](../../frontend/src/arkham/carcosaStoryArt.ts) points story keys to [the bundled WebP images](../../frontend/src/assets/carcosa-story). Both editions share a passage and illustration where their story is the same.

The story reference is the [Fantasy Flight Games Campaign Guide](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/58/cd/58cd918c-fdd0-4cee-99df-eb9b5fd43c13/ahc68_campaign_guide_v4-compressed.pdf), checked against the game’s [English story keys](../../frontend/src/locales/en/thePathToCarcosa) and the [Return to scenario handlers](../../backend/arkham-api/library/Arkham/Scenario/Scenarios/ReturnToThePathToCarcosa). Some scenes, such as the police desk, have no matching card illustration and use the story passage as their reference.

## Illustration selection

Use the passage’s decisive discovery, action, or consequence as the image’s focal point. Read the whole passage and its campaign-log effects before picking the scene. Share art when both passages depict the same pivotal moment. Give branches separate art when a choice changes the action or the discovery. Campaign-log checks and directions to another passage leave the illustration to the actual story passage.

The following story beats supersede the corresponding original prompts. The built-in ImageGen tool rendered the images as crisp, realistic 1536×1024 narrative illustrations using the referenced game card art for character, prop, and setting design.

## Original ImageGen prompts and card references

The [original Ward Theatre prologue](../../frontend/src/assets/return-to-carcosa-prologue.webp) is reused across both campaigns. The additional illustrations use the common prompt below, with the passage ID, card artwork and scene from each row supplied to ImageGen.

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Create one finished 1536 by 1024 landscape illustration for Fantasy Flight Games' Arkham Horror: The Card Game, The Path to Carcosa, based on the specific campaign-guide passage and actual in-game card references given here. Atmospheric 1920s period production still, lightly painted surfaces with sharp deep focus, clear silhouettes and strong local contrast, readable at 280 px width. Clear air. No image blur, blooming highlights, text, borders, graphic overlays, icons, logos, panels or card template. Preserve the character, costume, prop and setting identity visible in the supplied game card images, use only the artwork within them as visual reference. Recompose the requested story moment.
```
