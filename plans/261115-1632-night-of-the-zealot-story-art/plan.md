# Night of the Zealot story art

Scope: campaign prologue, all three scenarios’ narrative intros and resolutions, shared with Return to the Night of the Zealot. Routing prompts remain text only. No changes to translations, narration, or scenario rules.

Sources: `frontend/src/locales/{en,vi}/nightOfTheZealot`; campaign and scenario handlers under `backend/arkham-api/library/Arkham`. Style: `frontend/src/assets/carcosa-story/curtain-call-waking.webp`. Scene references: `frontend/public/img/scenes/night-of-the-zealot`. Lita Chantler 01117 and Ghoul Priest 01116: local scene actor art; Study 01111, Hallway 01112, Parlor 01115, Your House 01124, Rivertown 01125, Main Path 01149, Ritual Site 01156 and Umôrdhoth 01157: card definitions and corresponding Scene Maps where available.

Steps: generate individual plates → inspect at story width and export to WebP (1536 × 1024) → register passage keys with the shared story component → run focused tests and typecheck. The user checks the story panels in game.

The Midnight Masks’ `intro.body` reads the Campaign Log and leads to Intro 1 or 2. Its `resolutions.noResolution.body` leads to Resolution 1. The Gathering R3 leads to the new-allies intro; Midnight Masks R2 records that it is past midnight. The Devourer Below endings distinguish a failed campaign, an interrupted ritual, the banishment, and Lita's sacrifice.

## ImageGen prompts

Create one standalone 3:2 landscape story illustration for Arkham Horror LCG, 1536 × 1024 framing, full bleed. Cinematic period realism like the project's Carcosa story artwork: realistic depth and textured materials, subtle painterly finish, deep shadows with readable faces and focal action, restrained colors, practical warm light balanced with cold night, no glossy fantasy concept-art finish. Setting: Arkham, New England, 1925. If Lita appears: adult woman with chin-to-shoulder-length wavy copper-red hair, off-white collared blouse with sleeves rolled up, long dark charcoal skirt, plain sturdy lace-up boots; retain this costume. Do not depict named player investigators. No title, watermark, frame, game UI or legible text. Use a concise, readable single-scene composition.

- `prologue` → `hankersons-call.webp` (Friday September 18, 1925, oppressive heat in Arkham, Massachusetts. Tight over-the-shoulder view of an old soothsayer taking James Hankerson’s alarming call on a black candlestick telephone; still hand gripping the receiver, unfanned divination work on the desk, hazy view of tense New England street through open window. Discovery is conveyed by sudden stillness. No body or barn shown.)

The supernatural has not appeared yet. Printed papers are angled and illegible.)

Center Lita’s copper-red curls and cream blouse against the void. No gore, no wounds, no dismemberment.)
